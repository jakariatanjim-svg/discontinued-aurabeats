Why trust AuraBeats?

AuraBeats pulls music from open catalogs and public radio networks. Sources include artist-approved uploads, Creative Commons libraries, public-domain archives and live radio streams. Nothing requires an account, and the app keeps your playlists, favourites and listening history in your own browser.

  • Multiple Sources: JioSaavn, Audius, Jamendo, Archive.org.
  • Live Radio: Global network of thousands of radio stations.
  • Full Privacy: No accounts, no data collection, local storage only.
  • 100% Ad-Free: No banners, no pop-ups, no interruptions — ever.
  • High Quality: Streaming at up to 320kbps for crystal clear audio.
  • Artist Focused: Promoting open licenses and independent music.
  • Smart Search: Deep-scan across all supported databases instantly.
