Stream open music and live radio in one player

AuraBeats is a multi-source music player that aggregates JioSaavn, Audius, Jamendo, Internet Archive and live radio into one premium browser experience. It focuses on open streaming, artist-approved releases, Creative Commons music, archive recordings and instant search.

Why trust AuraBeats?

AuraBeats pulls music from open catalogs and public radio networks. Sources include artist-approved uploads, Creative Commons libraries, public-domain archives and live radio streams. Nothing requires an account, and the app keeps your playlists, favourites and listening history in your own browser.
